Context

Assetworks AI built an AI-driven research platform for investors, covering market analysis, news and insights. I owned the product lifecycle from discovery to launch. The company was later acquired by KoinBX.

The problem

Two things stood between an AI model and a useful research product. Its outputs were text-heavy, which made them slow to read and act on. And market questions need current data, which a model on its own doesn't have.

What I did

  • Replaced text-heavy outputs with visual HTML research reports, to improve comprehension and decision speed for traders and investors.
  • Architected a real-time financial data layer on MCP, pulling market data through API-driven systems to power insights.
  • Used feedback loops and behavioural analytics to find friction points, and balanced technical feasibility against user experience when prioritising.
  • Took the platform from discovery through launch to its first 1,500+ early adopters.

Results

  • Launched to 1,500+ early adopters.
  • Research delivered as visual reports instead of walls of text.
  • Insights grounded in real-time market data through the MCP layer.

What I learned

  • Format is part of the product. The same insight lands differently as a visual report than as a page of text.
  • For market questions, fresh data mattered as much as the model.

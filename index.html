About

Why customers do what they do

I studied Mechanical Engineering in Bengaluru and found my way into product by being the person who kept asking “why do customers do that?”. Since then I've worked at Meesho in its early hypergrowth days, rebuilt a doorstep service journey end to end at MFine and LifeCell, founded Peersclub, launched an AI research platform at Assetworks AI, and now run strategy and execution as Chief of Staff at KoinBX.

Outside work I build side projects with AI tools, and since 2024 I've coached aspiring PMs on breaking into product.
